Pattern setup

Tap ＋ when you finish a row — the big number is rows completed, the line under it is the row now on your needles. The ring is your stitch marker: one lap per pattern repeat, a tick per row. Pinned notes surface automatically when their row comes up. Every tap saves instantly (localStorage) — closing the tab or killing the battery loses nothing. Wake Lock needs a recent browser; if the button is dim, it isn't supported and the phone's display timeout applies.
