Bins

    Yards-per-row is an estimate: stitches × (basis ÷ stitch gauge) × fabric factor,
    converted to your unit. The default factor 3.0 suits stockinette; garter and crochet run higher —
    one measured row beats any factor, so enter it in Measured yd/row and it wins.
    Skeins = ceil(yards × 1.1 ÷ ball length) (10% margin) when ball-band data is set.
    Temperatures are pasted in by design — the tool never goes online.
    Plans live in this browser only (localStorage); the copied plan is the portable form.
