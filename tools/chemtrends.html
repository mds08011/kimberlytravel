Live points come from this device's Reading Queue log (read-only — nothing here modifies the queue). CSV imports use the queue's own export format and persist in chemtrends.v1, so history survives the queue's "clear entered". Shaded bands are the compliance ranges (FC: body minimum to 10 ppm; pH: 7.2–8.0; LSI: −0.3 to +0.3); red dots are out-of-band readings. Lines break across gaps longer than 3 days rather than inventing a trend. When a reading also logs alkalinity, calcium hardness, and CYA (Reading Queue v1.1.0+), the saturation index (LSI) is charted too, using Pump Room's exact formula. localStorage is per-device — this dashboard shows this device's logging history; the CSV import is the merge path across devices.
