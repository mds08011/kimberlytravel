Where the Number Comes From
Training prop for the same math Pump Room runs: LSI = pH + TF(temp) + (log₁₀CH − 0.4) + log₁₀(max(1, TA − CYA/3)) − 12.1 (−12.2 when TDS ≥ 1000 ppm). The TA − CYA/3 term is the carbonate correction — cyanurate alkalinity doesn't buffer calcium carbonate, so a third of the CYA reading is subtracted from TA before its factor is taken.
