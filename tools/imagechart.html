Chart

The image is drawn onto an offscreen canvas at exactly stitches × rows — the browser's own bilinear filtering is the downsample. In knitter's mode the row count is derived from the width and your stitch:row gauge, so the chart cells carry the rowG:stG aspect and the knitted result isn't vertically squashed. Colors are reduced by median-cut to your budget. Print uses the same true-physical-size approach as Graph Paper — printer at 100% / Actual size, never "Fit". Settings persist in imagechart.v1; the image itself is never stored and never uploaded.
