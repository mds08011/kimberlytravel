    Construction is drop shoulder, knit flat: body front and back are identical
    rectangles (front gets the neck drop), sleeves are tapered trapezoids sewn flat across
    the armhole edge. Stitch counts are inches × gauge rounded to even for
    cast-ons; rows are rounded to whole rows, so displayed counts can differ from the entered
    inches by up to half a stitch or row — knit the counts, not the ruler. The sleeve increase
    schedule uses the same overRows() math as Skein & Gauge, ported verbatim.
    Enter finished measurements — ease is your decision, made before this tool.
